How it is made

What we say, and where to check it

Ghee made from curd, the Bilona way

Our Gir Cow A2 Ghee starts as curd, not milk or malai, and is slow-churned by the Bilona method.

Stone pressed oils

Our oils are stone cold pressed and extracted without chemicals or solvents, as described on each oil.

Stone-ground atta

Buying from us

Delivery, returns & cancellations

Our terms, in plain words. No surprises after you pay.

Delivery

Standard shipping across India, usually delivered in 3–5 days depending on your location. Shipping charges, if any, are shown at checkout before you pay.

Returns & exchanges

Returns or exchanges within 2 days of delivery if the product is damaged, wrong, has a broken seal or arrived expired. It must be unused, in original packaging, with the invoice. Items bought during a sale or from the gifting category are not eligible for return or refund.

Cancellation

You can cancel any order that has not shipped yet, for a full refund. Please record an unboxing video. Any order discrepancy must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with that video.
