Once you're a member, 15% comes off on every order you place on rosierfoods.com. No codes to remember. It applies every time, for as long as your plan is active.

Simple maths. Two orders of about ₹1,000 each save you ₹300 at 15% off. That's already more than the ₹299 you paid for the 3-month plan. Everything after that is pure saving.
