Our traditional process

From Gir cow to your kitchen

असली स्वाद की एक सच्ची यात्रा

1

Gir desi cows

Milk from free-grazing indigenous Gir cows.

2

Set into curd

Milk is boiled, cooled naturally, then set into curd overnight.

3

Hand-churned bilona

The curd is churned the traditional Bilona way.

4

Lab tested

The ghee lab report is published for you to read.

5

To your table

Usually delivered in 3–5 days across India.
