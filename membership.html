Talk to us

It's our membership for families who shop with us often. You pay once for 3, 6 or 12 months, and you get 15% off on all orders, free & fast shipping, a birthday surprise box, member-only offers, priority support and a free 1L ghee on ₹15,000 orders.

Once you're a member, 15% comes off on every order you place on rosierfoods.com. No codes to remember. It applies every time, for as long as your plan is active.

Simple maths. Two orders of ₹1,000 each save you ₹300 at 15% off. That's already more than the ₹299 you paid for the 3-month plan. Everything after that is pure saving.

Once your orders during your membership add up to ₹15,000, we'll send you 1L of our ghee, free. It's our little thank-you for being family.

Add your birthday to your Rosier account after you join. When your birthday comes around during your membership, we'll send a surprise box to your door.

All plans get the same benefits. If you're trying us out, start with 3 months. Most families pick 6 months. And if Rosier's already in your kitchen every week, 12 months works out cheapest at just ₹83 a month.

Club benefits apply to orders placed on rosierfoods.com. That's where you'll see your member price, free shipping and rewards.

Your benefits stay active till the last day of your plan. You can renew anytime to keep saving, and choose a longer plan if you'd like to save more.

The refund rule for membership fees is still being confirmed by Rosier, so please check it at checkout. If something doesn't feel right, contact our team and we'll sort it out with you.
